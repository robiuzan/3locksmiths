# Keyword universe

The full addressable demand for this business, and which of it the site does and does not cover today.

`docs/keyword-map.md` describes **the 64 routes that exist** and the formulas they follow. This file
describes **everything that could exist**, ranked, so expansion is a decision rather than a guess.
Owned by the `keyword-strategist` agent; the gate on acting is
`docs/content-standards.md` §2 (the doorway test) and §7 (the expansion cap) below.

> **The one-line summary:** the site covers _planned_ demand (someone who already knows they want a
> key duplicated) and almost none of _urgent_ demand (someone locked out right now). Urgent is the
> higher-intent half of this trade.

## Tier C — Brand-key: the moat

18 live brands: קיה · פורד · סקודה · שברולט · סוזוקי · רנו · טויוטה · מאזדה · סיטרואן · פיג׳ו ·
יונדאי · מיצובישי · סובארו · מרצדס · הונדה · אופל · במוו · פולקסווגן

### C.1 Depth beats breadth — do this before adding brands

Each existing brand page can capture **dozens** of long-tail model queries without a new URL, by
adding model-and-year specificity (`/new-service` "depth bar"): which generations use which
transponder system, whether the car must be present, price band per key type per make.

Target queries this unlocks: `שכפול מפתח מאזדה 3` · `מפתח חכם יונדאי טוסון` · `מפתח קיה ספורטג׳` —
hundreds of variants, all served by 18 existing, already-ranking pages.

### C.2 Missing brands, ranked by Israeli road presence

**High priority (large fleets):** ניסאן · טסלה · BYD · צ׳רי · MG · סיאט · דאצ׳יה
**Medium:** אאודי · וולוו · ג׳יפ · לנד רובר · איסוזו · אלפא רומיאו · מיני
**Note:** the Chinese EV brands (BYD, צ׳רי, MG) are recent, high-growth, and almost certainly
under-served by competitors — the best new-page opportunity in this tier. **EV key systems differ;
confirm capability before publishing** 🔶.

## Tier D — Location

17 live cities. Deep, and they pass the doorway test.

**Geographic gaps, ranked by population × plausible service radius:**

**Tier D1 (central, high value):** אשדוד · רחובות · הרצליה · רמת השרון · הוד השרון · ראש העין ·
מודיעין · נס ציונה · לוד · רמלה · קרית אונו · אור יהודה · יבנה
**Tier D2 (further):** אריאל · נצרת · טבריה
**Tier D3 (only if genuinely serviced):** אילת · צפת · דימונה

> ⛔ **Do not target ירושלים, באר שבע, אשקלון, בית שמש, כרמיאל, עכו or עפולה.** All seven had
> location pages and all seven were **withdrawn 2026-09-02 — the business does not service them**
> (`docs/business-facts.md` §E.1). They 301 to `/אזורי-שירות/`. Re-adding any of them as a keyword
> target would rebuild a page for work the business cannot do, which is the "leads it cannot
> fulfil" failure §E exists to prevent. That is also why Tier D3 is worded the way it is.

### D.1 The sub-city opportunity

For the three largest cities, neighbourhood pages can outrank city pages for local intent —
**but only where three or more genuinely local facts exist** (`/new-city`):

- **תל אביב:** פלורנטין · נווה צדק · רמת אביב · הצפון הישן · יפו · הבורסה
- **חיפה:** הדר · אחוזה · נווה שאנן · קריית אליעזר
- **ראשון לציון:** רמת אליהו · נווה ים · קרית ראשון

(ירושלים's neighbourhoods were listed here until 2026-09-02 and are gone with the city — §E.1.)

### D.2 The highest-value location play: emergency × city

`פתיחת רכב נעול בתל אביב` is far more urgent — and converts far better — than
`שכפול מפתח בתל אביב`. Build for the **top 5 cities only**, after Tier E exists.

## Tier E — Emergency / lockout — 🟡 CORE THREE SHIPPED 2026-08-25

> ✅ `פתיחת רכב נעול`, `פתיחת דלת נעולה` and `מפתח נשבר במנעול` are live. Deliberately three, not
> six — the lockout phrasings share intent and separate pages would be a doorway cluster.
> `מנעולן חירום / 24 שעות` remains **unbuilt**: pure availability claim, hours still 🔶.
> `ילד ננעל ברכב` is handled as safety content inside the car page (call 100/101 first), not as a
> page targeting the query.

Was zero coverage. This is the highest-intent demand in the entire trade: the searcher is standing next
to a locked car or door, will call the first credible result, and does not compare prices.

| Query                          | Urgency      | Note                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| `נעלתי מפתחות ברכב`            | extreme      | the canonical lockout query        |
| `פתיחת רכב נעול`               | extreme      | service page                       |
| `פתיחת דלת נעולה`              | extreme      | home equivalent                    |
| `ננעלתי מחוץ לבית`             | extreme      | conversational variant             |
| `מנעולן דחוף` / `מנעולן עכשיו` | extreme      | modifier on head term              |
| `אבד לי המפתח היחיד לרכב`      | high         | also the best guide topic (Tier F) |
| `מפתח נשבר במנעול`             | high         | distinct service                   |
| `מפתח נשבר בסוויץ׳`            | high         | car-specific                       |
| **`ילד ננעל ברכב`**            | **critical** | see the warning below              |
| `כלב ננעל ברכב`                | critical     | same treatment                     |

> ⚠️ **`ילד ננעל ברכב` needs editorial care, not keyword optimisation.** A page targeting it must
> lead with "call 100/101 immediately" before anything commercial. Publishing a sales page against a
> child-endangerment query is both a reputational and an ethical failure. Treat it as a genuine
> safety page that happens to rank, or do not build it.

**Emergency pages also need honest availability.** Do not build `מנעולן 24 שעות` while the schema
says 08:00–18:00. Fact first (`docs/business-facts.md` §D.3).

## Tier F — Question / informational (the guides hub)

Zero coverage — the entire topical-authority and AEO gap. Each answers better than the service page,
then links to it for the commercial action (`/guides-hub`).

**Highest value, in build order:**

1. `כמה עולה שכפול מפתח לרכב` — a real breakdown by key type, not a range restatement
2. `אבד לי המפתח היחיד לרכב מה עושים` — highest-intent question in the trade
3. `מה ההבדל בין מפתח עם שבב למפתח חכם` — the comparison table competitors lack
4. `אפשר לשכפל מפתח רכב בלי המקור` — high volume, direct answer
5. `כמה זמן לוקח לשכפל מפתח רכב`
6. `מתי צריך להחליף צילינדר ומתי מנעול שלם`
7. `מה עושים כשמפתח נשבר בתוך המנעול`
8. `איך בוחרים מנעול לדלת כניסה` — buyer's guide
9. `מה זה מנעול רב בריח ומתי צריך אותו`
10. `האם הביטוח מכסה פתיחת רכב נעול` 🔶 needs factual care — do not state policy terms

## Tier G — Commercial modifiers

Applied to any of the above: `מחיר` · `מחירון` · `כמה עולה` · `מומלץ` · `ביקורות` · `קרוב אליי` ·
`זול` · `מיידי`

- **`מחירון`** — `/מחירון/` exists. It is the natural magnet for every `כמה עולה` query, and should
  link to and from every guide.
- **`ביקורות` / `מומלץ`** — **blocked**. Cannot be targeted honestly with zero reviews
  (`docs/business-facts.md` §B). This is another reason the Business Profile is the critical path.
- **`קרוב אליי`** — won by the Google Business Profile and proximity, not by a page.

## Tier H — B2B / segment 🔴 uncontested

No competitor in this space markets to organisations. Each is a page:

`מנעולן לוועד בית` · `מנעולן לחברת ניהול נכסים` · `מנעולן לצי רכב` · `מנעולן למוסך` ·
`מנעולן לחברת ליסינג` · `מנעולן לעסקים` · `מנעולן לסוכנות רכב`

B2B leads are higher-value and recurring. The copy angle is different: response SLA, invoicing,
multiple sites, key management — not "we'll be there fast".

## 7. The expansion cap — the rule that stops this becoming spam

Ordered. Do not skip.

1. ✅ **Done** — `content/enriched/95.mjs` (the Tier-1 head term).
2. **Tier E emergency cluster** — the biggest gap and the highest intent.
3. **Tier F guides hub** — topical authority, and it feeds every other tier.
4. **Tier C depth** on the 18 existing brand pages — hundreds of long-tail queries, zero new URLs.
5. **Tier H B2B** — uncontested, high value.
6. **Tier B2 gaps** (safes, smart locks, access control) — each gated on a capability 🔶.
7. **Tier C new brands** — EV brands first.
8. **Tier D new cities**, then emergency × city for the top 5.
9. **Tier D sub-city** neighbourhoods — last, and only where the doorway test genuinely passes.

**Hard limits, in force at every step:**

- **Never build the service × location matrix** (30 × 17 = 510). A templated grid is a doorway
  cluster and the penalty lands on the domain.
- **Never publish a page for a service the business does not perform**, or a city it does not serve.
  Every 🔶 in this file is a `docs/business-facts.md` question before it is a page.
- **Never target a review or availability term the facts do not support.**
- Depth on an existing page beats a new page, every time, on this site.
